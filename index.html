Chicago Pinball Expo 2026: Machine Map

Expo hall

Huge thanks to MN Retro Gamer, Cary Hardy and the Dutch Pinball Museum, whose walkthrough videos made this map possible. Every machine was checked frame by frame against their footage and what they say on camera, and each one links back to the moment it appears. Go watch and subscribe.

Tap the free play area for 180+ machines row by row, the Homebrew Village for 30+ one-of-a-kind machines built by hobbyists (with links to their builders), or a numbered booth for what vendors and manufacturers have on show. Counts are what we could identify; there's more on the floor than we could see.

Homebrew Village

Machines built by hobbyists and small studios, in the order MN Retro Gamer walks past them. Builder names and links come from the builders' own pages, Pinside, Kineticist and the shows they've been at. "Not found" means we looked and couldn't confirm it. Photos are from the builders or from videos about each machine.
