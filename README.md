# Chicago Pinball Expo 2026: Machine Map

A fan-made map of the pinball machines at the Chicago Pinball Expo 2026 (Schaumburg, IL, October 14 to 17, 2026): the free play rows, vendor booths and the Homebrew Village.

**View it:** https://rincs1979.github.io/chicago-pinball-expo-2026/

Machines were identified from public walkthrough videos by Cary Hardy, MN Retro Gamer and the Dutch Pinball Museum, with game details and photos from [OPDB](https://opdb.org), the Open Pinball Database. Homebrew photos and links belong to their builders.

Not affiliated with Pinball Expo. Spot a mistake? Open an issue.
